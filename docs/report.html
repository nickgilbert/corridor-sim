Glossary — terms & symbols

The architectures compared

ModelWhat it is
A — incumbentTraditional ramp, off-chain FX venue. Denomination-mismatched (fiat + USDC), days-long rebalance, idle (0% yield) inventory. No returns to scale.
B — stable/stableDenomination-matched local stablecoin ↔ USDC on an on-chain DEX. Seconds-long rebalance, but 0% yield inventory.
C — reserved rampYield-bearing tokenized treasuries. C-xborder = yield↔yield (e.g. CETES↔USTRY, both legs earn). C-same-denom = yield↔stable (e.g. USTRY↔USDC, one yield leg).

9 · Network: hub-and-spoke compounding

Corridors funnel local value through tokenized US Treasuries (USTRY) as a shared reserve; USDC is just the dollar-exit spoke. Two claims, across MX_MXN and corridors added in order (MXN→BRL→KRW→TRY).

Point 1 — yield is universally cheaper. Delivering a dollar asset, C (local→USTRY) is ≤ B (local→USDC) by the yield subsidy and ≪ A in every corridor.

Point 2 — it compounds, fragmented stable-to-stable does not. Cross-border cost falls toward a floor as corridors aggregate at the unified USTRY hub. A steelman stable-to-stable competitor routes through a USDC hub; with one unified stablecoin it nearly keeps up (losing only by yield), but with a stablecoin per market its liquidity can never consolidate — turnover per pool stays flat, β never nets — so its cost flatlines while C keeps falling, and the gap widens with the network.

11 · Assumptions & caveats

The yield edge is an edge over a risk, not a riskless gift (V6 §7.6). The model surfaces, rather than hides, the open questions:
  • VaR vs the yield edge. The sub-bps advantage must cover the value-at-risk of holding securities rather than cash. An optional var_charge term (default 0) subtracts directly from the yield credit — the key honest unknown.
  • Interest-rate risk on short-duration treasuries (low, not zero): sensitivity to a sharp yield drop is included in the tornado via the y_local/y_ustry ranges.
  • Bond-market liquidity / hours. Can seize in a crisis; stress via a higher f_redeem and longer on-chain settlement preset.
  • Friction differential. f_redeem·β must remain below the yield edge ΔS for the model to hold; trials breaching this are economically the failure mode. In the persistent-imbalance MX_MXN cell at T=10×, 100.0% of reserved-ramp trials breach it (p_friction_breach is recorded per cell in results_summary.csv).
  • Imbalance is the adversary, not turnover alone. ΔS shrinks as 1/T, so C's win is largest exactly in the thin, imbalanced, early-corridor conditions SDF subsidises.

A — Denomination-mismatched fiat+USDC, off-chain venue FX, days-long rebalance, idle (0% yield) inventory. No returns to scale.

B — Local stablecoin matched to fiat, on-chain DEX FX, seconds-long rebalance, idle stablecoins (0% yield). Returns to scale, capped at cash-FX parity.

C_xborder — Tokenized local treasury vs tokenized USD treasury (e.g. CETES<->USTRY). Both legs earn carry; yield funds a quote below cash-FX parity.

C_same_denom — Tokenized USD treasury vs USD stablecoin (USTRY<->USDC). Hub-and-spoke: the yield leg sits on one side of every trade (~50% of inventory always earning).
